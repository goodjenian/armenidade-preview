AGBU Young Professionals Brasil

  • São Paulo, SP
  • Part of União Geral Armênia de Beneficência · UGAB Brasil
@ypbrasil

Young professionals group linked to UGAB/AGBU in Brazil, part of a global network for Armenians aged about 22 to 40. It organized the FOCUS 2019 meeting in São Paulo with AGBU headquarters and holds career talks.

Activities

  • Talks on professional careers
  • Networking events
  • Organization of AGBU FOCUS (2019, São Paulo)
  • Charity and fundraising events
