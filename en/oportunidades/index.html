Birthright Armenia

Volunteer and cultural exchange program for young people of the diaspora, founded in 2003, based in Yerevan and also operating in Gyumri and Vanadzor.

See details
  • Main program (volunteering/internship): Customized internships by field, minimum 30 hours/week, from 9 weeks to 1 year.
  • Birthright Lite: 4 to 9 weeks for professionals with full-time jobs; different financial rules.
Program website@birthrightarm

ExchangeInternshipEntrepreneurshipVolunteeringEngage Armenia 2026

AGBU

The largest nonprofit organization dedicated to Armenian heritage, founded in 1906; in Brazil it operates as UGAB Brasil.

  • Discover Armenia: 15–18; GLP: 19–26
See details
  • Discover Armenia: 10-day or 3-week trip through the regions of Armenia with community service; 2026: spring April 3–12, summer 1 June 24–July 12, summer 2 July 27–August 14. Fee US$ 1,000 (spring) / US$ 2,000 (summer), airfare not included.
  • Global Leadership Program (GLP): Boston and New York (8 weeks, US$ 5,000) and Yerevan (~6 weeks, June 20–July 31, 2027, US$ 3,000); application fee US$ 65; partial/full scholarships based on need.
  • Young Professionals (YP): Global network for ages 22–40; in Brazil, YP Brasil.
  • AGBU Business Mentors and ANI Program (English conversation, virtual): Business mentoring and virtual volunteering (according to Estação Armênia).
Program website@agbuglobal
EducationVolunteeringEngage Armenia 2026

Armenia Education Initiative

Education reform organization that runs Teach For Armenia, recruiting local and diaspora talent to teach in schools in rural communities.

    See details
    • Teach For Armenia – National Leadership Program: 2-year fellowship teaching in high-need schools, with a 5-week virtual pre-service and a 7-week Teacher Leadership Academy in the summer.
    • Researcher/mentor: Partnership in public policy or technical mentoring (according to Estação Armênia).
    Program website@armeniaeducationinitiative

    Health For Armenia

    NGO inspired by Teach For Armenia that trains and sends family doctors to rural regions of Armenia.

    • Up to 55 years old
    See details
    • Healthcare Leadership Program: 2 years with training (Healthcare Academy) and mentoring; stipend of 100,000 AMD/month (local doctors) or 150,000 AMD/month (relocated), according to Estação Armênia.
    • Support volunteering: Events, fundraising, marketing and teaching.
